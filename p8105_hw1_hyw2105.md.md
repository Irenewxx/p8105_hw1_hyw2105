``` r
data("penguins", package = "palmerpenguins")
```

The `penguins` dataset contains measurements of penguins observed in the
Palmer Archipelago, Antarctica. It has 344 observations and 8 variables.

The dataset includes penguins from three species (Adelie, Chinstrap,
Gentoo) living on three islands (Biscoe, Dream, Torgersen). Important
variables include bill length (`bill_length_mm`), bill depth
(`bill_depth_mm`), flipper length (`flipper_length_mm`), body mass
(`body_mass_g`), `sex`, and `year`. Observations were collected from
2007 to 2009.

The mean flipper length is 200.92 mm.

``` r
penguins_scatterplot =
  ggplot(
    penguins,
    aes(
      x = bill_length_mm,
      y = flipper_length_mm,
      color = species
    )
  ) +
  geom_point()
```

The scatterplot shows a generally positive relationship between bill
length and flipper length, although the three penguin species form
distinct clusters. Adelie penguins tend to have shorter bills and
flippers, Gentoo penguins generally have the longest flippers, and
Chinstrap penguins tend to have relatively long bills but intermediate
flipper lengths. The warning indicates that observations with missing
